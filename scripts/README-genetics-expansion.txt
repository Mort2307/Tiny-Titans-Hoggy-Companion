Tiny Titans species genetics expansion rules

1. Every species is identified by its accepted scientific name and stable species id.
2. Genetics are species-scoped. No trait is borrowed from another species unless the catalogue explicitly maps it.
3. A missing genetics catalogue means baseline-only, not "no morphs exist".
4. A catalogue with reviewed traits is partial until its relevant genetics are sufficiently mapped and evidenced.
5. Combination names are phenotype labels; they do not become independent loci.
6. Super forms are represented by increased copy counts when the underlying inheritance model supports that interpretation.
7. Polygenic, line-bred and unresolved traits must not receive artificial Mendelian probabilities.
8. Aliases resolve to a canonical identity; display names never become genetic identity keys.
9. Source-backed traits retain their evidence source so later audits can distinguish catalogue data from verified individual lineage.
10. The same canonical composition model must feed Morph Library, saved snake genetics, parent selection, breeding calculation and morph reversal.
