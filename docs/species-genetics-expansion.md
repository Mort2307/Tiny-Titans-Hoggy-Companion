# Tiny Titans species genetics expansion

Every registered species is explicitly classified as `mapped`, `partial`, or `baseline-only` for genetics coverage.

Traits are species-scoped and must carry a stable identity, canonical name, aliases, inheritance model, het capability where applicable, and evidence/source metadata.

Combination names are phenotype labels, not independent loci. Super forms are copy-count states where supported. Polygenic, line-bred, disputed, and unresolved traits do not receive artificial Mendelian probabilities.

The same canonical composition model must feed Morph Library, saved snake genetics, parent selection, breeding calculation, and morph reversal.

Expansion order: finish Hognose regression coverage, then complete the highest-demand captive snakes already registered, followed by specialist species only where evidence is strong enough to avoid inventing genetics.
