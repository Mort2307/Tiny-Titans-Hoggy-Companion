# Tiny Titans Intelligence Product Roadmap

## Product direction
Tiny Titans should feel like a professional keeper command centre: fast to log, calm to navigate, evidence-led, and transparent about uncertainty.

## Intelligence hierarchy
Observation → Pattern → Signal strength → Confidence → Actionability → Evidence → Suggested next observation.

## Creator Centre hierarchy
System Health → Data Health → Product Intelligence → Action Queue.

## Priority improvements
1. Replace repeated Creator N+1 reads with pre-aggregated intelligence snapshots.
2. Add historical intelligence snapshots so Creator Centre can compare 7/30/90/365 day periods.
3. Separate privacy minimums from statistical confidence.
4. Add evidence, freshness, bias-risk and eligibility to benchmark outputs.
5. Add behavioural user cohorts and onboarding funnels.
6. Add anomaly detection and data-quality queues.
7. Feed Tester evidence into release-readiness intelligence.
8. Add progressive disclosure to long tracker forms.
9. Consolidate shared snake loading and CRUD workflow patterns.
10. Keep individual intelligence as the primary keeper experience; population intelligence remains contextual and privacy-controlled.
